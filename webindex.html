About

I work across security controls assessment, third-party risk assessments, Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs), and cybersecurity policy analysis. At Accenture, I'm a Transformation Excellence Consultant within Cloud Strategy and Advisory (Technology Strategy & Transformation), with a portfolio in cybersecurity: the space I'm most interested in.

Beyond GRC work, I'm bringing my data analytics background into practice, building tools that make risk work more accessible, measurable, and efficient. I care about learning from and with others. I've mentored junior risk assessors on assessment methodologies, stakeholder interviews, and evidence validation, recognized as 2025 Q3 Professional Mentor Champion at Accenture's Technology Strategy & Transformation Town Hall.

I'm moving intentionally toward the intersection of technology risk, AI risk, and cyber governance, most recently completing ISACA's AI Risk Management Bootcamp at the CIAG Conference 2026, with a particular interest in third-party and vendor AI risk. Open to conversations and opportunities in that space.
